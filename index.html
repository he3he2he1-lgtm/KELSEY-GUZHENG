Meet the Guzheng

Dating back over 2,500 years, the guzheng has been part of Chinese musical tradition for centuries. It was once played alongside instruments such as the pipa and xiao in imperial court music. Today, it continues to evolve and find new life in modern music.

Three Songs

  1. 01

    Adapted from Mia & Sebastian’s Theme from La La Land.

  2. 02

    Dance of the Yi People, presumably based on traditional music of the Yi people of southern China.

  3. 03

    A musical fusion of YCK’s Bad News with three Chinese folk melodies: Dance of the Yi People, Mo Li Hua (Jasmine Flower), and Dance of the Yao People.
